2. AI-Generated Content

Where content is labeled or reasonably understood to be AI-generated, it does not depict an actual, real person and therefore does not depict a "performer" within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 2257. Our Terms of Service separately and absolutely prohibit AI-generated content that depicts, simulates, or evokes a real, identifiable minor, or that uses the likeness of a real, identifiable adult without that person's consent, regardless of whether § 2257 applies to that content as a technical matter.

Every submitting party represents and warrants, as a condition of using the upload feature, that:

  • Every person appearing in the submitted content was an actual adult, at least 18 years of age, at the time the content was created;

Content is removed immediately upon any credible indication that these representations are false, and the associated account is subject to suspension and referral to law enforcement or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
