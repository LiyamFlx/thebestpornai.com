Character Creator & Memory in Practice

Candy AI's creator pairs granular anatomy and personality sliders with a fast preview loop, so dialing in a specific look takes minutes rather than credit-burning trial and error. In our 7-day run, characters recalled names, preferences, and storyline beats set on day one without re-prompting — the strongest cross-session memory we recorded this benchmark cycle.

Identity preservation was the other standout: the same face held through pose changes, outfit swaps, and on-demand selfies without the drift that plagues cheaper diffusion stacks. That consistency is most of what separates a 9.9 quality score from the mid-9s.

Video & Voice in Practice

Live Action video clips render with smooth keyframed motion and run longer than companion-first rivals like OurDream's 30-second cap. Heavy video rendering is also the main token sink on Premium, so plan generation batches accordingly. Voice messages carry realistic pitch and emotional inflection rather than flat text-to-speech, and arrive quickly enough to keep a conversation moving.
