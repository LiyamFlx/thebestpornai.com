How much does Xotic AI cost per month?

Xotic AI costs $14.99 / mo for entry-level access, scaling up to premium tiers ranging from $29.99 to $49.99 per month for unlimited fast-lane generation, 4K video exports, and priority GPU queues.

Subscribers who commit to annual billing pay roughly half the standard monthly rate. All plans renew automatically until canceled in user account settings.

Does Xotic AI have a free trial or free tier?

Yes, Xotic AI provides a functional free allowance without requiring credit card details upfront. Free users receive 10 free credits upon verified email signup. However, high-demand features like 4K upscaling, persistent multi-week memory, and long-form video diffusion require an active paid subscription tier.

What shows up on your credit card statement for Xotic AI?

Xotic AI charges appear under the neutral billing descriptor XT COMPUTING. To safeguard subscriber confidentiality, bank and card statements will never display adult or explicit keywords.

How do you cancel a Xotic AI subscription?

You can cancel Xotic AI online in under 60 seconds by visiting Profile → Membership → Cancel Subscription. Once canceled, your premium access will remain active until the end of your prepaid billing period, with zero cancellation fees or penalty charges.
