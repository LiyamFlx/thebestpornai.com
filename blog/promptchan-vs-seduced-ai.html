Frequently Asked Questions

Is Seduced.ai better than PromptChan?
For photorealism and skin textures, Seduced.ai is significantly better (9.7 vs 9.4). For anime, hentai, and stylized 2D/3D art, PromptChan is the superior platform.
Which is cheaper between PromptChan and Seduced.ai?
PromptChan offers a free starter tier and paid plans from $14.99/month, whereas Seduced.ai has no free tier and starts at $24.00/month.
