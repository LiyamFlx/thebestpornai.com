Companions vs. Image Generators vs. Curated Watching

Privacy & Safety Checklist Before You Subscribe

Uncensored does not mean unregulated. Before handing any companion or image platform a payment method, check for:

  • Encrypted chat storage — top-tier platforms encrypt conversation logs and offer a way to delete history entirely.
  • Discreet billing — statement descriptors that do not reveal the platform name or content type.
  • Clear non-consent policies — a published stance against generating real, identifiable people without consent.
  • Transparent renewal terms — visible cancellation flow and no silent tier upgrades.

Every platform ranked above publishes at least a baseline privacy policy; read it once before your first payment rather than after a billing dispute.
