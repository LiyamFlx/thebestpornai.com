1. What Is an AI Video Generator?

An AI video generator is a generative-AI system capable of creating or transforming video based on user-provided inputs. Unlike a conventional video editor (where creators manually arrange clips and apply transitions), an AI video pipeline generates pixels and movement programmatically from descriptions and image seeds.

USER INPUT │ ├── Text prompt (action, environment, camera motion) ├── Reference image (character identity & starting pose) ├── Motion intensity / Camera trajectory settings └── Video generation parameters (FPS, Duration, Seed) │ ▼ AI TEMPORAL MODEL (e.g. SVD / AnimateDiff / Proprietary) │ ├── Scene interpretation ├── Optical flow & motion vector synthesis ├── Frame sequence latent diffusion └── Temporal attention & consistency smoothing │ ▼ COHERENT VIDEO SEQUENCE (5s – 120s)

2. How AI Video Generation Works

Video is a continuous sequence of visual frames (typically 24 to 60 frames per second). If each frame looks impressive individually but the character's facial structure or lighting changes abruptly between frames, the result feels unnatural. AI video architectures solve this by adding temporal attention layers that calculate spatial relationships across adjacent frames.

FRAME 1 ──► FRAME 2 ──► FRAME 3 ──► FRAME 4 ──► FRAME 5 │ │ │ │ │ └───────────┴───── Temporal Attention ─────┴───────────┘ │ COHERENT MOTION

3. Text-to-Video

Text-to-video starts with a natural language description specifying the subject, setting, action, camera movement, and visual aesthetic. The temporal model generates both the visual elements and their motion trajectory simultaneously.

4. Image-to-Video

Image-to-video begins with a still photograph or rendered image. The model uses the source image as Frame 0 and animates the subject according to motion vectors or prompt instructions. This is currently the most reliable method for maintaining character identity.

5. How AI Creates Motion

A video model calculates optical flow fields—predicting which pixels should displace, rotate, or deform from one frame to the next. The system must coordinate foreground subject movements (e.g. walking, dancing, breathing) with background camera movements (panning, zooming, tracking).

6. Temporal Consistency

Temporal consistency refers to the stability of physical objects, character faces, clothing textures, and lighting across consecutive frames. Without robust temporal attention, videos suffer from flickering, morphing, or ghost limbs.

7. Character Consistency in Video

Maintaining character identity in a video is substantially more complex than in still images. The character must turn their head, change facial expressions, and interact with objects while their underlying facial bone structure and body proportions remain locked.

Platforms like Candy AI achieve extended Live Action video generation up to ~120 seconds by locking latent seed states and applying fine-tuned facial conditioning across the temporal diffusion pipeline.

9. Why AI Video Generation Is Difficult

Video requires models to understand both 3D spatial geometry and temporal physics simultaneously. A 10-second video at 30 FPS requires the generation of 300 perfectly synchronized frames—a massive computational challenge that demands high GPU VRAM and specialized model architectures.

10. Generation Time and Cost

Because video synthesis is compute-heavy, generation times range from 30 seconds to several minutes per clip. Platforms charge higher credit/token amounts for video than for still images. When evaluating platforms, calculate the cost per usable minute of video rather than looking solely at the base monthly subscription price.

11. AI Video Editing

Modern video suites offer capabilities beyond initial generation, including video extension (chaining clips for longer scenes), video-to-video style transfer, and camera motion path adjustments.

12. Privacy and Data Considerations

Video generation involves uploading reference images or generating sensitive personal scenarios. Verify that the platform uses encrypted video storage, discrete billing descriptors, and guarantees that user video generations are not used for public model training.

16. Frequently Asked Questions

How do AI video generators work?
AI video generators use temporal diffusion models that generate frame sequences while applying temporal attention to keep subjects, lighting, and camera motion consistent over time.
Why is AI video harder than image generation?
An image only needs to look coherent for a single static instant. A video must maintain physical structure, identity, and fluid motion across hundreds of frames without flickering or morphing.
What is temporal consistency?
Temporal consistency is the degree to which visual elements (faces, bodies, backgrounds) remain stable and free of distortion from one video frame to the next.
