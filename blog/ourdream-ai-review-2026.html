Character Creation & Customization

The character creator is where OurDream earns its 9.4 customization score. You can build a companion from scratch — face, body type, style, personality traits, and backstory — or start from the public discovery feed and adjust an existing character. The same identity then carries across chat, stills, and video, which is the platform's core advantage: you are not re-rolling a new face every time you switch formats. The step-by-step walkthrough lives in Can I Create My Own Character for AI Sex Chat on OurDream.ai?, and this guide covers how image and video generation stay locked to one character.

The Lust Level slider deserves its own mention. Instead of coaxing the model to escalate through prompt phrasing, you set an intensity dial per conversation and the roleplay tone follows it. In our sessions it proved more reliable than text-based escalation cues, especially mid-scene — it is the feature we most wish competitors would copy.

Video & Voice in Practice

Video clips run 5–30 seconds with lip-sync and stay on the character face you chat with. Motion is smooth for conversational clips, though anyone wanting long standalone video diffusion should look at Candy AI's Live Action engine instead. Who Should Skip OurDream

Skip OurDream if your primary use is standalone image generation with no companion layer — Seduced.ai gives you tighter pose control for that job. Skip it if you need clips longer than 30 seconds; Candy AI's Live Action engine runs longer sequences. And if you want a native mobile app, OurDream is browser-only by design — the mobile web app is capable, but nothing installs on your home screen.
