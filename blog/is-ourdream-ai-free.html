Frequently asked questions

Can I use OurDream.ai for free indefinitely?

You can browse characters for free. Unsubscribed chat costs 1 coin per message. When signup tokens run out, messaging stops until you subscribe or add coins.

Do Dreamcoins expire if I don't use them?

No hard expiry date. Monthly subscription grant coins do not roll over (unused grant is replaced on renewal). Purchased top-up packs stay until spent.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

The account reverts to free status. Remaining coins stay, but chat costs 1 coin per message again and uncensored/unblurred tools lock.

Does OurDream.ai offer full NSFW?

Yes, for active 18+ paid members. Uncensored roleplay and unblurred generation are gated behind a subscription.

Can I pay with PayPal?

Not at the time of writing. Checkout lists cards, crypto, and G2A.
