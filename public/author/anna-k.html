Who writes these reviews

Anna is 28 and based in New York City. She has spent the last several years testing generative AI tools — image diffusion, video synthesis, and companion chat — and now runs the review desk at thebestpornai. She writes under a pen name, which is the reality of putting a byline on adult-industry reviews; the testing behind each score is real, documented, and repeated on every platform the same way.

Her testing rules are simple: every platform is evaluated on a paid account bought like any customer's, every score uses the same six weighted criteria, and privacy claims are verified against the live product — billing descriptors, deletion flows, and retention policies — not the marketing page. Platforms she hasn't fully reviewed yet carry a directory score only and are marked "Not yet reviewed" on the 2026 leaderboard.
